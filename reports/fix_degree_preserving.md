# Fix: degree-preserving graph control (23 Nov 2026)

## Issue
The independent review noted that the edge-shuffle control `shuffle_edges_within_step` (src/preprocessing.py) draws both endpoints of every edge uniformly at random within its time step. That keeps only the per-step edge counts. It changes the degree of most nodes, so the log-degree features change too, and the real-versus-shuffled comparison mixes the effect of topology with the effect of degree. We built a configuration-model-style control that keeps every node's in-degree and out-degree, and we reran the experiments that depend on the control.

## Method
All code is in `experiments/degree_preserving_control.py`. It reuses `src.preprocessing`, `src.model.make/tune_lgbm`, `src.fusion` and `run_experiments.novelty/BLOCKS`, and modifies no shared file. Within each time step, the destination endpoints are randomly permuted across that step's edges while the source endpoints stay fixed. This preserves every node's out-degree and in-degree exactly. Self-loops and duplicate directed edges are then repaired. Each faulty edge swaps its destination with a randomly chosen edge of the same step, and the swap is accepted only if it creates neither a self-loop nor an existing directed edge. A swap of destinations never changes any degree. The rewiring rng is `np.random.default_rng(2024)`. The neighbourhood view is computed on the rewired graph with `pp.neighbourhood_view`, giving the view Gdp. LightGBM was tuned on AF+Gdp with the grid and split used in `run_experiments.py` (train 1–29, validate 30–34). For seeds 42, 1, 7, 123 and 2024 the script produced cross-fitted out-of-time predictions for the four blocks 15–19, 20–24, 25–29 and 30–34, and a final fit on 1–34 scored on the test steps 35–49. These are saved as method `LGBM-AF+Gdp`. The reliability gate was fitted over the experts LGBM-AF (existing predictions) and LGBM-AF+Gdp in the same way as TRUST-GRAF in `experiments/refit_fusion.py`: all seven reliability features, fitted on OOT steps 15–29, with l2 = 0.001 taken from `results/gate_lambda_choice.json`. As in TRUST-GRAF and A-shuffled-graph, the structural gate inputs (log-degrees and dispersion) come from the real graph. The gate predictions are saved as method `A-dp-graph-gate`, with the `alpha` and `w` arrays.

Commands, each run under the shared lock:
`flock /tmp/cpu.lock python3 experiments/degree_preserving_control.py train` (610 s),
then `python3 experiments/degree_preserving_control.py valcheck`, which reads only the calibration block 30–34,
then, after the prediction files were frozen (their SHA-256 digests are in `results/dp_control_frozen_preds.json` and are re-checked before scoring), `flock /tmp/cpu.lock python3 experiments/degree_preserving_control.py test`.
A synthetic-graph unit test is available as `... selftest`.

## Verification of degree preservation (`results/dp_control_rewiring.json`)
The script asserts that every node's in-degree and out-degree after rewiring equal the originals, and both assertions passed. It also asserts that every edge stays within its time step, and that the log-degree columns of Gdp are identical to those of G; both passed as well. Of the 234,355 rewired edges, 0.71% coincide with an original directed edge and 0.74% with an original undirected pair. The initial permutation produced 59 self-loops and 1,289 edges in duplicate groups. One repair pass with 782 accepted swaps removed all of them, so the rewired graph has 0 self-loops and 0 multi-edges (234,355 distinct directed pairs). The rewiring creates 58 reciprocal edge pairs; the original graph has none. For comparison, the old uniform shuffle changes the in-degree of 67.2% of nodes and the out-degree of 68.4%, and it leaves 45 self-loops and 42 excess multi-edges.

## Validation sanity check (block 30–34 only, `results/dp_control_valcheck.csv`)
The seed-mean out-of-time PR-AUC on the calibration block was 0.9928 for LGBM-AF+G, 0.9916 for LGBM-AF, 0.9908 for LGBM-AF+Gshuf and 0.9900 for LGBM-AF+Gdp. The tuning-time validation PR-AUC of AF+Gdp was 0.9902, with selected parameters num_leaves 31, min_child_samples 20 and 907 trees (`results/dp_control_hyperparameters.json`). The order matches the existing pipeline, and no anomaly was seen. The fitted gate is degenerate: its intercept lies between 8.7 and 9.7 in every seed, and the mean alpha is at least 0.9998. It therefore routes almost all weight to the node-only expert. A-dp-graph-gate is effectively LGBM-AF and matches it to four decimals on both validation and test.

## Test results (`results/dp_control_results.csv`, `results/dp_control_metrics_per_seed.csv`)
Platt scaling and the F1-optimal threshold were fitted on OOT 30–34 with the `src.evaluation` helpers, as in `statistical_tests.py`. Per-seed metrics for the existing methods match `results/metrics.csv` exactly (maximum difference 0). Seed-mean test PR-AUC / F1 are as follows. LGBM-AF scores 0.8016 / 0.8013. LGBM-AF+G scores 0.8020 / 0.8202. LGBM-AF+Gshuf scores 0.7966 / 0.8131. LGBM-AF+Gdp scores 0.7984 / 0.8092. TRUST-GRAF scores 0.8026 / 0.8109, and A-dp-graph-gate scores 0.8016 / 0.8013. All methods have F1 after step 43 near 0.03, and LGBM-AF+Gdp scores 0.0278 there.

The paired stratified bootstrap used 1,000 resamples within time step, the seed-averaged metric and rng 12345. LGBM-AF+G versus LGBM-AF+Gdp gives ΔPR-AUC = +0.0037 [0.0013, 0.0061] and ΔF1 = +0.0111 [0.0052, 0.0170]. LGBM-AF+Gdp versus LGBM-AF gives ΔPR-AUC = −0.0033 [−0.0051, −0.0014] and ΔF1 = +0.0079 [0.0042, 0.0117]. Both comparisons have p_boot = 0.001, which is the floor of 1/B, and Holm-adjusted p = 0.002 across the two primary comparisons. The p-values are therefore bounds, not exact values. Two secondary comparisons were not Holm-corrected. LGBM-AF+Gdp versus LGBM-AF+Gshuf gives ΔPR-AUC = +0.0017 [−0.0002, 0.0035] with p = 0.069, and ΔF1 = −0.0039 [−0.0079, −0.0002] with p = 0.039. TRUST-GRAF versus A-dp-graph-gate gives ΔPR-AUC = +0.0010 [0.0002, 0.0019] and ΔF1 = +0.0096 [0.0060, 0.0133].

## Interpretation
Against a null that keeps degrees, the real neighbourhood view carries some signal beyond degree. G and Gdp have identical degree features and differ only in which nodes are neighbours, and G is better on both metrics: +0.0037 PR-AUC and +0.011 F1. The size of this gap needs care, for the same reason as the shuffled control. Most of the PR-AUC gap comes from the degree-matched random view harming ranking: −0.0033 versus no graph view. The real view's PR-AUC gain over no view is only +0.0004, which `statistical_results_secondary.csv` reports as not significant. In F1 the degree-matched random view still improves on node-only by +0.008, about 40% of the real view's +0.019 F1 gain. A further 60% of the real view's F1 gain needs the actual who-pays-whom structure. A random view with matched degrees therefore raises the thresholded F1 without true topology, possibly because the extra features or the larger tuned model change the operating point. Preserving degree hardly changes the old conclusions: Gdp and Gshuf are statistically indistinguishable in PR-AUC, and Gdp's F1 is slightly lower. The defensible statement is this. On PR-AUC, the real topology beyond degree gives no measurable gain over using no graph view. On F1 it gives a modest gain over a degree-matched null, but part of the F1 advantage of a graph view does not depend on topology. With the degree-preserving expert, the reliability gate collapses to the node-only expert.

Several limitations remain. Only one rewiring realisation (seed 2024) was used. The bootstrap resamples transactions and ignores clustering in the graph, so the intervals are probably too narrow. The creator-supplied aggregate features f94–f165 are computed on the real graph and appear in every view, so this control tests only the recomputed neighbourhood view, not the total graph signal. The rewiring adds 58 reciprocal pairs, which is negligible.

## Verdict
The control issue is resolved. A degree-preserving control now exists, is verified to keep the degrees exactly, and has been run through the full protocol with five seeds. The manuscript must now describe both controls accurately. It should report that the real view beats the degree-matched null, with ΔPR-AUC +0.0037 and ΔF1 +0.011. It should also report that the degree-matched null itself reduces PR-AUC and raises F1 relative to node-only. The lead still has to integrate these numbers and decide whether `statistical_tests.py` should include the two new methods, which it will discover automatically from `results/preds`.

## Repeated rewiring draws

The round-2 reviewer noted that the degree-preserving control rested on one rewiring realisation (rng 2024). A real-versus-rewired gap from one draw mixes rewiring randomness with signal. We therefore generated two more independent rewirings, with rng seeds 7 and 99. The code is in `experiments/dp_control_draws.py`. It imports `degree_preserving_rewire`, `verify` and `ap_fast` from `experiments/degree_preserving_control.py` and does not modify that file. Each new draw passed the same assertions as the original: every node's in-degree and out-degree are unchanged, every edge stays within its time step, and the log-degree columns of the rewired view equal those of the real view. Both draws needed one repair pass. Draw 7 started with 52 self-loops and 1,226 edges in duplicate groups and was repaired with 731 swaps. Draw 99 started with 50 self-loops and 1,219 duplicate-group edges and was repaired with 733 swaps. Both final graphs have 234,355 distinct directed pairs, with no self-loops and no multi-edges. They retain 0.71% (draw 7) and 0.70% (draw 99) of the original directed edges, and they contain 52 and 58 reciprocal pairs (`results/dp_draws_rewiring.json`).

For each new draw, LGBM-AF+Gdp was trained with the hyperparameters fixed in `results/dp_control_hyperparameters.json` (num_leaves 31, min_child_samples 20, 907 trees), without re-tuning, for model seeds 42, 1, 7, 123 and 2024. As instructed, the full four-block cross-fitting was not repeated. Each seed got only two fits. A fit on labelled steps 1–29 scored calibration block 30–34, and a final fit on steps 1–34 scored the test steps 35–49. The block-30–34 prediction is defined exactly as the corresponding cross-fitted block in the original run, because that block's training set was also all labelled steps before 30. The predictions are in `results/preds_dp_draws/LGBM-AF+Gdp_rng{7,99}_seed*.npz`, kept out of `results/preds` so that `statistical_tests.py` does not discover them. Their SHA-256 digests were frozen in `results/dp_draws_frozen_preds.json` before any test metric was computed. As a reproducibility check, the script re-ran draw 2024 with seed 42. The rewired edge list matched the original digest, and both the block-30–34 and test predictions matched `results/preds/LGBM-AF+Gdp_seed42.npz` exactly (maximum absolute difference 0). Test metrics used the standard procedure: Platt scaling and `best_f1_threshold` from `src.evaluation`, fitted on OOT 30–34. Rescoring the existing rng-2024 draw reproduced `results/dp_control_metrics_per_seed.csv` to within 1e-9. The script ran in two stages under the shared lock. `flock /tmp/cpu.lock python3 experiments/dp_control_draws.py train` took 562 s. `flock /tmp/cpu.lock python3 experiments/dp_control_draws.py score` took under a minute. Logs are in `logs/dp_draws.log`, `logs/dp_draws_train.log` and `logs/dp_draws_score.log`.

The results are in `results/dp_draws_summary.csv`, with per-seed rows in `results/dp_draws_metrics_per_seed.csv`. The seed-averaged test PR-AUC / F1 of LGBM-AF+Gdp is 0.7984 / 0.8092 for draw 2024, 0.7978 / 0.8072 for draw 7 and 0.7962 / 0.8047 for draw 99. LGBM-AF+G scores 0.8020 / 0.8202. The real-minus-rewired gap in seed-averaged PR-AUC is +0.0037, +0.0042 and +0.0058 for the three draws, with mean +0.0046 and range [+0.0037, +0.0058]. In F1 the gap is +0.0111, +0.0131 and +0.0156, with mean +0.0132 and range [+0.0111, +0.0156]. The pairs were formed by matching the model seed. The real view is ahead in all 15 of the 15 draw × seed pairs in PR-AUC, with per-pair gaps from +0.0025 to +0.0072. It is also ahead in all 15 pairs in F1, with gaps from +0.0020 to +0.0345. Across draws, the standard deviation of the seed-averaged rewired score is 0.0011 in PR-AUC and 0.0022 in F1. This is small relative to the gaps but not negligible. The original draw 2024 happens to give the smallest gap of the three, so the single-draw estimate in the sections above was conservative, not favourable.

The direction of the real-versus-rewired comparison is therefore stable across rewiring draws. No draw or draw × seed pair reverses it, in either metric. The magnitudes reported above (+0.0037 PR-AUC, +0.011 F1) are the lower end of the three-draw range, and the three-draw means are +0.0046 and +0.013. The two secondary patterns noted earlier also hold in every draw. Each degree-matched null lies below node-only LGBM-AF in PR-AUC (0.7962–0.7984 against 0.8016) and above it in F1 (0.8047–0.8092 against 0.8013). The interpretation in the earlier section is therefore unchanged. The real view's own PR-AUC gain over no graph view remains only +0.0004, so most of the PR-AUC gap still comes from the rewired view harming ranking rather than the real view helping it. Three draws are a small sample of the rewiring distribution, and these comparisons carry no formal test across draws. The bootstrap intervals in the earlier section cover only one draw, so they do not include rewiring variance. The issue is resolved to the extent that the sign of the effect is shown to be robust to the rewiring realisation. The manuscript should report the three-draw mean and range rather than the single rng-2024 value.
